Try-On Bench

A test of live body tracking and true-to-scale garment drawing on this phone. It draws an example T-shirt at its real measurements, so a size that is too small looks too small.

  1. Enter the wearer's height.
  2. Face the camera with head and hips in the picture. The shirt appears at an approximate true size straight away.
  3. For an exact size, step back once so head and feet are both in the picture. Then move as you like.
